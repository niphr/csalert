<p class="cs-section">Features</p>

<div class="cs-cards">
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">01</div><h3>Complete the weeks still arriving</h3><p>Late reports make the newest weeks look too low. A nowcast engine completes them as Monte-Carlo draws, so the uncertainty travels on.</p></div>
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">02</div><h3>Trend, intensity and alerts, on the draws</h3><p>The recent slope, five MEM intensity levels and a historical-baseline alert each run on every draw. So the reporting uncertainty reaches the alert.</p></div>
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">03</div><h3>One table at the end</h3><p>The last step collapses the draws to quantiles, in a tidy <code>csfmt_rts_data_v3</code> for tables and figures. No analysis runs after it.</p></div>
</div>
