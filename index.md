<p class="cs-section">Features</p>

<div class="cs-cards">
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">01</div><h3>Complete the weeks still arriving</h3><p>Late reports make the newest weeks look lower than they will end up. An engine fills those weeks in and hands back draws rather than a single number, so the uncertainty travels onward instead of being thrown away at the first step.</p></div>
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">02</div><h3>Trend, intensity and alerts, on the draws</h3><p>Recent slope and the probability of increase, five-level MEM intensity, and exceedance of a historical baseline each take the completed draws and return them. Reporting uncertainty reaches the trend and the alert instead of stopping at the nowcast.</p></div>
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">03</div><h3>One table at the end</h3><p>When the analysis is finished, collapse the draws to quantiles. That step is terminal: it yields a tidy <code>csfmt_rts_data_v3</code> for tables, figures and storage, and nothing analytical runs on a collapsed table.</p></div>
</div>
